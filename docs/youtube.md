## Description
```
AI agents are starting to shop for us, and pay with our PayPal. But checkouts are built to trick: add-ons already ticked, items slipped into the basket, fees that appear only at payment, money nobody explains. An $11.49 coffee becomes $21.95, and an AI agent won't notice.

PennyWise for PayPal sits between your agent and your money. The AI proposes. PennyWise checks. PayPal moves only the money you approved.

How it works
• You set the rules: what to buy, the most to spend, which fees are fine (an intent mandate).
• A Gemini agent shops the store one page at a time. It has no tool that can pay.
• PennyWise reads every page, numbers each dark pattern, and blocks any charge outside your rules. The checker is plain code, not AI.
• Only you can approve extra fees. Then PennyWise signs a cart mandate for the exact amount.
• PayPal authorizes and holds the money, PennyWise reads the order back line by line, and only then is it captured. Any mismatch, and it's voided.
```
